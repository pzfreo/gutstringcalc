Tension from the Mersenne–Taylor law, T = ¼πd²ρ(2Lf)². Downbearing on the bridge is 2T·cos(θ/2), where θ is the included angle between the two string segments either side of the bridge. Gauges are the plain-gut diameter; for a wound string read it as the equivalent gut gauge. Pure intervals are chained outwards from the A nearest the reference pitch, so a long chain of just fifths drifts from equal temperament — that is the tuning, not a rounding error.
